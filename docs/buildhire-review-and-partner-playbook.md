# BuildHire Review & Partnership Playbook

This playbook supports BuildHire’s local authority, referral pipeline and backlink acquisition. It is designed for use only after genuine equipment-hire interactions. **Do not fabricate reviews, offer incentives for positive reviews, filter customers based on sentiment, or publish customer names, project details or photographs without written approval.**

## 1. Review-request workflow

Send one request after the equipment has been collected and the account contact confirms the job is complete. The same request should be available to every eligible customer, regardless of whether the expected feedback is positive or negative.

| Timing | Channel | Purpose | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collection day + 1 | WhatsApp or SMS | Request a genuine Google review while the experience is current. | BuildHire operations |
| Collection day + 7 | Email | Send one polite follow-up only if no review has been left. | BuildHire operations |
| After any complaint is resolved | Email or WhatsApp | Invite honest feedback once the resolution is complete; never make a review conditional on the outcome. | BuildHire owner |

### WhatsApp/SMS template

> Hi {{first_name}}, thanks for hiring with BuildHire for {{job_or_suburb}}. If you have a minute, we would value an honest review of your experience: {{google_business_profile_review_link}}. Your feedback helps local trades choose equipment hire with confidence. Thank you — BuildHire.

## 3. Partner-link request after a verified working relationship

Only send this to a trade business that has completed at least one genuine hire or is already working collaboratively with BuildHire.

**Subject:** Add BuildHire to your supplier resources?

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Thanks for working with BuildHire. If you maintain a supplier or project-resources page on your website, would you consider listing BuildHire as an equipment-hire option for Sydney / NSW projects?
>
> Suggested listing:
> **BuildHire — dry-hire excavators and construction equipment for Sydney and NSW projects.**
> https://buildhire.com.au/
>
> In return, we are happy to consider including a link to your business in relevant educational content where there is a genuine resource fit. No obligation—just let us know.
>
> Regards,
> {{sender_name}}

## 4. Monthly operating cadence

| Week | Activity | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send eligible post-hire review requests. | Requests sent, reviews received, average rating. |
| 2 | Personalise and send 3–5 association / partner enquiries. | Replies, meetings, listings or sponsorship options. |
| 3 | Follow up once with non-responders and publish one genuinely useful trade resource. | Qualified referral sessions and new referring domains. |
| 4 | Update the organic-to-hire report. | Leads, quote value, confirmed hires and gross profit by landing page / source. |
